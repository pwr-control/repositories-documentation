2. Predictive current loop seen as a deadbeat loop (no vector limit, one axis)
   g = (true voltage / predicted voltage)*(L_prediction / L_true).
   Before: no delay compensation -> e(k+1) = e(k) - g*e(k-1), poles z^2 - z + g = 0.
   before, q axis  : g = 2.591, |z| = 1.610 (unstable, oscillation at 1599 Hz)
   before, d axis  : g = 3.120, |z| = 1.766 (unstable, oscillation at 1636 Hz)
   With g = 1 (right voltage base, no delay compensation) |z| = 1: undamped oscillation at f_s/6.
   check, g = 1  : |z| = 1.000, oscillation at 1335 Hz
   After: delay compensated (current of now predicted with the vector of the last step)
   -> e(k+1) = (1 - g)*e(k-1), poles z^2 = 1 - g: the error is multiplied by 1 - g every two steps.
   after, q axis   : g = 1.000, 1 - g = +0.000, |z| = 0.000 (deadbeat for g = 1)
   after, d axis   : g = 1.204, 1 - g = -0.204, |z| = 0.452 (deadbeat for g = 1)

4. Gate timing of the T-type legs on the tc grid of the Simscape local solver (gate 0/15 V, filter [tc 1],
   CMOS logic threshold 7.5 V and Q1-Q4 interlock in dead_time_leg_generator_1, switch threshold 5.5 V).
   The command changes at a control instant; the bridge sees the gates at t0 + k*tc.
   before the fix (no turn-on delay):
      O -> P: Q3 off, Q1 on (Q2 on)     : Q3 off from step 2 (1.250 us), Q1 on from step 1 (0.625 us) -> SHORT CIRCUIT of a capacitor at step 1
      P -> O: Q1 off, Q3 on (Q2 on)     : Q1 off from step 1 (0.625 us), Q3 on from step 1 (0.625 us) -> no overlap
      O -> N: Q2 off, Q4 on (Q3 on)     : Q2 off from step 2 (1.250 us), Q4 on from step 1 (0.625 us) -> SHORT CIRCUIT of a capacitor at step 1
      N -> O: Q4 off, Q2 on (Q3 on)     : Q4 off from step 1 (0.625 us), Q2 on from step 1 (0.625 us) -> no overlap
   after the fix (turn-on delay 3 us on the four gates):
      O -> P: Q3 off, Q1 on (Q2 on)     : Q3 off from step 2 (1.250 us), Q1 on from step 6 (3.750 us) -> no overlap
      P -> O: Q1 off, Q3 on (Q2 on)     : Q1 off from step 1 (0.625 us), Q3 on from step 6 (3.750 us) -> no overlap
      O -> N: Q2 off, Q4 on (Q3 on)     : Q2 off from step 2 (1.250 us), Q4 on from step 6 (3.750 us) -> no overlap
      N -> O: Q4 off, Q2 on (Q3 on)     : Q4 off from step 1 (0.625 us), Q2 on from step 6 (3.750 us) -> no overlap
   Before the fix the first step after O -> P / O -> N saw Q3 (Q2) still at 15*exp(-1) = 5.52 V (on) and Q1 (Q4)
   already on: one step of short circuit of a 14.4 mF capacitor per change. After the fix every switch turns on
   3 us later, after the one that turns off has gone below 5.5 V (two steps, 1.25 us).
