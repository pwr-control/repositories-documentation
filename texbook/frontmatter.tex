\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This volume collects a set of technical notes written independently over
time, each one originally a standalone document. They are grouped here into
five parts: the physics and behaviour of the power semiconductor devices
themselves; the Simscape models built to represent those devices in circuit
simulation; gate drivers and firing circuits; complete converters and their
applications; and finally measurement, standards and system-level analysis.

The notes were not conceived as chapters of a single book, so their depth and
style vary: some are design guidelines, some are model documentation, some are
worked examples on a specific machine or installation. Cross references between
chapters have been added where the material overlaps, and all bibliographies
have been merged into a single list at the end of the volume.

A few chapters are still in Italian, in the form in which they were first
written. They are marked in the table of contents by their titles only; the
technical terminology is English throughout, so they should remain readable
alongside the rest.

\vspace{5mm}
\noindent\emph{Note on notation.} The source documents were written at different
times and do not always agree on symbols. Where a conflict was unavoidable the
original notation of each chapter has been kept and defined locally at first
use, rather than rewriting derivations that were checked in their original form.
