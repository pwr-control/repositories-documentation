1. Per-unit chain (one of the six systems)
   machine: ubez = 365.8 V peak, ibez = 486.0 A peak, tau_bez = 143.06 kNm, omega_bez = 96.93 rad/s (15.43 Hz), 1 pu power = 266.7 kW
   Rs = 30.10 mOhm, Ld = 6.24 mH (0.8036 pu), Lq = 7.56 mH (0.9736 pu), Ls = (Ld + Lq)/2 = 0.8886 pu; with the 230 uH inductor the bridge sees 0.8333 pu (d) and 1.0033 pu (q)
   battery: 332 cells, open circuit 1249 V at soc 0.85 (1.168 pu of udc_nom 1070 V), R0 = 72 mOhm, R1 = 72 mOhm, C1 = 10 mF
   Before the fix the predictive control computes the vectors from vdc = 1 (Constant) without any voltage
   scale: 2/3 for the vector [1 0 0], in pu of udc_nom, while its model needs pu of ubez.
   The true vector is 2/3*udc/ubez = 2.277 pu: the prediction sees the voltage 3.42 times too small.
   EKF voltage before the fix: 3/2*u*u_psm_scale_ekf = 3/2*u*1.6889 -> 1.689 pu for [1 0 0], 0.742 x the true voltage, and the vector of the last step (one step too new).
   Fixed predictive control: state voltages from the measured vdc (pu of udc_nom) times u_scale = udc_nom/ubez = 2.9252 -> pu of ubez;
   prediction and EKF with L_norm = (Lq + LFi)/Lbez = 1.0033 pu and R_norm = (Rs + RLFi)/Xbez = 0.0424 pu (before: Ls_norm = 0.8886, Rs_norm = 0.0400);
   the EKF gets the voltage of the vector of two steps before (output of the predictive control = vector of the
   last step, then the Unit Delay of mp control), gains 1, u_psm_scale_ekf = 1.
   rated speed: torque curve 125.0 kNm per system -> iq_ref = -0.874 pu (id = 0), bridge voltage 476 V peak = 1.30 pu of ubez = 0.66 of the linear limit udc/sqrt(3)
   With L_norm = Lq + LFi the flux of the EKF is the "active flux" psi_m + (Ld - Lq)*id: it lies on the d axis for
   any id, so the EKF angle has no saliency error (with Ls it lagged by about atan((Lq_t - Ls)*iq/psi_m)).
   Saliency error of the EKF before the fix at rated torque: -5.7 deg
   Angle delay of the chain: the EKF angle used at step k is the one of t(k-2) and the vector acts until t(k+1):
   3 steps = 1.11 deg at rated speed, i.e. id = -0.017 pu for the rated iq (not compensated, small).

2. Predictive current loop seen as a deadbeat loop (no vector limit, one axis)
   g = (true voltage / predicted voltage)*(L_prediction / L_true).
   Before: no delay compensation -> e(k+1) = e(k) - g*e(k-1), poles z^2 - z + g = 0.
   before, q axis                : g = 3.025, |z| = 1.739 (unstable, oscillation at 3057 Hz)
   before, d axis                : g = 3.643, |z| = 1.909 (unstable, oscillation at 3120 Hz)
   before, q axis, bridge 1 mH on: g = 2.681, |z| = 1.637 (unstable, oscillation at 3012 Hz)
   With g = 1 (right voltage base, no delay compensation) |z| = 1: undamped oscillation at f_s/6.
   After: delay compensated (current of now predicted with the vector of the last step)
   -> e(k+1) = (1 - g)*e(k-1), poles z^2 = 1 - g: the error is multiplied by 1 - g every two steps.
   after, q axis                 : g = 1.000, 1 - g = +0.000, |z| = 0.000 (deadbeat for g = 1)
   after, d axis                 : g = 1.204, 1 - g = -0.204, |z| = 0.452 (deadbeat for g = 1)
